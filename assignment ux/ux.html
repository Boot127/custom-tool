Our Journey

A timeline of our basketball CCA program

2010: Inception

Nanyang Poly Basketball CCA was established.

2015: First Tournament Win

Our competitive team won the regional polytechnic championship, bringing home Nanyang Poly's first basketball trophy.

2018: Facility Upgrade

The polytechnic invested in new basketball courts with professional flooring and training equipment, significantly improving our training environment.

2021: National Recognition

Our competitive team reached the national semi-finals, earning Nanyang Poly a place among the top polytechnic basketball programs.

2025: Present Day

With over 150 active members and 6 qualified coaches, we continue to develop student-athletes who excel both on the court and in the classroom.

Our Mission & Values

At Nanyang Poly Basketball, we believe basketball is more than just a game - it's a vehicle for developing well-rounded individuals. Our program focuses on:

  • Excellence: Striving for personal bests in both athletics and academics
  • Teamwork: Learning to work collaboratively toward common goals
  • Respect: For teammates, opponents, coaches, and officials
  • Discipline: Developing commitment and work ethic
  • Sportsmanship: Winning with humility and losing with grace
